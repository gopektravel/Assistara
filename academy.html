INSIDE ASSISTARA ACADEMY

From learning to earning.

In four focused weeks, build the skills, proof and client-acquisition system to start pursuing paid virtual assistant work. Learn the work, practice it, prove it and take your service to market.

WEEK 01

Build the foundation.

Professional communication, remote-work standards, core virtual assistant workflows, Google Workspace, AI and the tools businesses expect you to use.

Outcome: handle day-to-day client work.
WEEK 02

Build your value.

Choose your direction, deepen the skills behind your service and complete realistic simulated client work.

Outcome: know what you can sell and why it matters.
WEEK 03

Build your proof.

Create your portfolio, define your service offer, sharpen your positioning and build a professional profile.

Outcome: show proof before asking to be hired.
WEEK 04

Start finding clients.

Learn prospecting, outreach, discovery calls, proposals, onboarding and how to manage a client relationship professionally.

Outcome: know how to pursue paid opportunities yourself.
