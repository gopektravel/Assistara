Email Command Center

A single view of every transactional and campaign email across Resend, Brevo and Sender. Sending acceptance is always measured; delivery, bounces and complaints come from signed provider webhooks, and opens/clicks are measured first-party by Assistara. Opens are estimated (image proxies and blocking reduce accuracy) and scanner clicks are excluded from engagement. Nothing here is back-filled.
