How the quiz works

Ten questions, one screen each, and the answers are scored on the server against seven remote virtual assistant career paths. Your report names the path that fits you, the day to day work and tools it involves, and a four-week plan scaled to the hours you have. Every income figure is an illustrative calculation, your hours multiplied by an example hourly rate, with the formula shown so you can check it yourself.

This is a careers assessment, not a psychometric test, and it is part of Assistara Academy. No compatibility percentage is calculated or shown. Nothing here is required to see your result, and completing Assistara Academy does not guarantee employment.
