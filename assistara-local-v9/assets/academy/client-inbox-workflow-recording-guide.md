# Build a Client Inbox Workflow - Recording Guide

**Target length:** 9-11 minutes

1. Open with the real outcome: a busy inbox becomes clear actions and reliable follow-through.
2. Explain that client preferences come before labels, filters or inbox rules.
3. Teach the four decisions: reply now, draft for review, turn into a task, file/archive.
4. Demonstrate the four-step workflow on one realistic email thread: read the full thread, check urgency, choose the owner, record the next action.
5. Contrast a vague reply with a clear reply that names the action and next update.
6. Flag the common errors: changing rules without approval, replying to sensitive messages without approval, leaving no owner/next action, rushing without context.
7. Direct students to complete the six-message simulation and the knowledge check.

Use the teaching deck in order. Keep the demo grounded in the fictional scenario; do not show real client messages or personal data.
