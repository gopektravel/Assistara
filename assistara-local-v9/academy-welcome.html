How the Academy works

We teach the program through live sessions with the cohort.

After each live session, the recording and content are added to the relevant classes inside the Academy.

That means some classes may not have a video yet simply because that live session hasn't happened yet. You can still explore whatever materials are already available.

Can't make a live session? No problem. Every session is recorded, so you can catch up afterward.
