Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Assistara (www.getassistara.com) is operated by Assistara Inc. We match businesses with trained virtual assistants. Contact: hello@getassistara.com.

2. Data we collect

We collect data you provide directly (name, email, company, form responses) and data collected automatically (IP address, browser type, pages visited, referral source, device information).

3. How we use your data

  • To deliver our services (matching, onboarding, communications).
  • To send relevant updates and marketing (with your consent).
  • To measure advertising performance (Google Ads, Reddit Ads) and improve our site.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Advertising & analytics

We use Google Ads and Reddit Ads to measure campaign effectiveness. These platforms may set cookies and process identifiers (e.g., hashed email) for conversion tracking and advanced matching only after you give consent via our cookie banner. You can withdraw consent at any time via the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We share data with processors who assist us (Supabase for database, Resend/Brevo for email, Stripe for payments, Google/Reddit for ads measurement) under data processing agreements.

6. Retention

We retain personal data while your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with laws, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Analytics cookies expire after 13 months; consent preferences persist for 1 year.

7. Your rights (GDPR / Philippines DPA)

  • Access, rectify, or erase your personal data.
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Data portability.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing/advertising cookies.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

To exercise these rights, email hello@getassistara.com.

8. International transfers

Your data may be processed outside the Philippines/EEA (e.g., US-based processors). We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and adequacy decisions where applicable.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (encryption in transit/at rest, access controls, regular reviews). No internet transmission is 100% secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted here with a revised date. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

Questions? Email hello@getassistara.com or write to Assistara Inc., 123 Business Ave, Makati, Philippines.
